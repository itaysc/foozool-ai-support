# Foozool AI Support - Quick Overview

## 🎯 **What We Do**
AI-powered customer success platform that automates support, generates insights, and drives customer growth through intelligent analysis of tickets, documents, and customer interactions.

---

## 🚀 **Core Capabilities**

### **🤖 Autonomous AI Support**
- **Smart Ticket Analysis**: AI automatically analyzes tickets for sentiment, urgency, and complexity
- **Automated Actions**: Auto-refunds, coupons, replies, escalations based on customer tier and confidence scores
- **Risk Management**: Daily limits, audit logging, and configurable thresholds prevent abuse

### **📊 50+ AI Insights**
- **Customer Health**: Health scoring, churn prediction, engagement tracking
- **Support Intelligence**: Recurring problems, sentiment trends, resolution optimization
- **Business Growth**: Upsell opportunities, expansion signals, capacity planning
- **Stakeholder Analysis**: Decision maker mapping, engagement patterns, influence tracking

### **📁 Document Intelligence**
- **AI Document Analysis**: Automatic classification, sentiment analysis, topic extraction
- **Meeting Prep**: Auto-create Google Docs with professional templates
- **Content Insights**: Extract action items, feature requests, and business relevance from all documents
- **Google Drive Integration**: Seamless sync and processing of Google Workspace files

### **🎫 CRM Integration**
- **Multi-CRM Support**: Works with Zendesk, Salesforce, HubSpot, and others
- **Unified Interface**: Single dashboard for all CRM systems
- **Real-time Sync**: Webhook integration for instant ticket updates
- **Customer 360°**: Complete customer view with health scores and interaction history

### **🔍 Advanced Search**
- **Vector Search**: Semantic search across tickets, documents, and files using Qdrant
- **Context-Aware Results**: Intelligent search with relevance scoring
- **Multi-Modal Search**: Search across all content types simultaneously
