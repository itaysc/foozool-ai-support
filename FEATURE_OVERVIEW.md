# Foozool AI Support - Feature Overview

## 🎯 **System Overview**
Foozool AI Support is a comprehensive customer success and support automation platform that combines AI-powered insights, CRM integration, document management, and autonomous support capabilities to help organizations deliver exceptional customer experiences.

---

## 📊 **INSIGHTS & ANALYTICS**

### **Customer Success Insights**
- **Health Score Calculation**: Comprehensive 0-100 health scoring based on support health (40%), engagement health (30%), and business health (30%)
- **Risk Assessment**: Automated risk detection for churn, declining engagement, and customer satisfaction issues
- **Stakeholder Analysis**: Track engagement levels, influence patterns, and decision-making power across customer organizations
- **Ticket Pattern Recognition**: Identify recurring problems, escalation trends, and resolution time patterns
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Monitor customer sentiment trends and detect satisfaction declines
- **Usage Analytics**: Track feature adoption, utilization rates, and growth patterns
- **Meeting Intelligence**: Analyze meeting summaries, extract action items, and track customer satisfaction scores

### **Predictive Analytics**
- **Churn Prediction**: Early warning system for customers at risk of churning
- **Expansion Opportunities**: Identify upsell and cross-sell opportunities based on usage patterns
- **Resolution Time Prediction**: Predict ticket resolution times using ML models
- **Volume Anomaly Detection**: Statistical analysis to detect unusual spikes or drops in ticket volume
- **Sentiment Anomaly Detection**: Baseline comparison to identify sentiment deviations and trends

### **Data Intelligence**
- **Real-time Metrics**: Live dashboards with customer health, ticket volumes, and performance indicators
- **Trend Analysis**: Historical data analysis with trend identification and forecasting
- **Performance Monitoring**: Track KPIs, SLAs, and operational efficiency metrics
- **Custom Reporting**: Configurable reports and analytics for different stakeholder needs

---

## 🤖 **AUTONOMOUS AI & AUTOMATION**

### **AI-Powered Ticket Analysis**
- **Intelligent Classification**: Automatic ticket categorization, sentiment analysis, and urgency detection
- **Confidence Scoring**: AI confidence levels for automated decision-making
- **Intent Recognition**: Understand customer intent and suggest appropriate responses
- **Complexity Assessment**: Evaluate issue complexity and estimated resolution time

### **Automated Actions**
- **Smart Refunds**: Automatic refund processing based on customer tier and issue type
- **Coupon Generation**: Dynamic discount coupon creation for customer satisfaction
- **Auto-Reply System**: Intelligent automated responses to common inquiries
- **Auto-Resolution**: Automatic ticket closure for resolved issues
- **Priority Escalation**: Smart escalation based on customer tier and issue severity
- **Action Thresholds**: Configurable rules for when and how AI should take actions

### **Risk Management**
- **Daily Action Limits**: Prevent abuse with configurable daily action caps
- **Customer Tier System**: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers with different permissions
- **Audit Logging**: Complete tracking of all automated actions for compliance
- **Confidence Thresholds**: Configurable minimum confidence levels for automated actions

## 📁 **DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT & ANALYSIS**

### **Document Processing**
- **Multi-Format Support**: Handle meeting summaries, notes, reports, links, and Google Docs
- **AI-Powered Analysis**: Automatic document classification, topic extraction, and sentiment analysis
- **Content Intelligence**: Extract key topics, action items, and business relevance scores
- **Folder Organization**: Hierarchical folder structure with customer-specific organization
- **Batch Processing**: Efficient bulk document analysis with pre-classification filtering

### **Google Drive Integration**
- **Seamless Sync**: Direct integration with Google Drive for document access
- **Real-time Processing**: Process Google Docs, Sheets, and Presentations automatically
- **OAuth2 Authentication**: Secure Google account integration
- **Document Creation**: Create new Google Docs with professional templates
- **File Type Detection**: Automatic MIME type detection and appropriate processing

### **Vector Search & Retrieval**
- **Semantic Search**: Advanced vector-based search using SBERT embeddings
- **Context-Aware Results**: Intelligent search results based on document context and relevance
- **Quality Scoring**: Automatic embedding quality assessment for search optimization
- **Multi-Collection Support**: Separate collections for documents, tickets, and Google files
